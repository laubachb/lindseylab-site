Chemistry and materials under extreme conditions

The Lindsey Lab in the Department of Chemical Engineering at the University of Michigan studies chemistry in inherently multiscale systems and material evolution under extreme and dynamically changing conditions, using advanced simulation and machine learning.

Research areas

Chemistry at extreme conditions

Multiscale simulations of shock- and pressure-driven chemistry: detonation, carbon condensation and nanoparticle growth, phase transformations, equations of state, and thermochemistry far from ambient conditions.

Machine-learned interatomic models

Development of the ChIMES many-body interaction models and the active-learning workflows that train them, giving near-quantum accuracy at the time and length scales of reactive molecular dynamics.

Data science for materials

Machine-learning tools to interpret large experimental and simulation datasets, quantify training-set coverage, and build material diagnostic and performance models.

Soft and nanostructured materials

Transferable many-body models for complex soft matter, including polymer-grafted nanoparticles, and machine-learning tools for nanomaterial design and discovery.
